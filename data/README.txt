Aria local data directory. Everything here stays on your computer.

Place your complete 1.09 GB archive here as:
    wiki-eng-simple.zim

Aria will create, as needed:
    wiki_cache.sqlite3    Cached Wikipedia passages (up to 512)
    data.json             Crutch's local notes, lists and other saved data
    other Crutch local files such as timetable.json / unmatched.log

The Wikipedia archive is NOT included in the download.
Migrate existing notes or your archive with: python migrate_old_data.py --copy-notes --move-zim
